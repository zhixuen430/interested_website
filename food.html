Below are some affordable Malaysian local dishes you must try,along with hidden restaurants and their reviews to help you compare and choose the best place to eat.

Local Cuisine

Nasi Lemak

Nasi lemak is Malaysia's national dish, featuring fragrant coconut milk rice served with spicy sambal, anchovies, peanuts, cucumber, and boiled egg.

Laksa

Laksa is a spicy Southeast Asian noodle soup, typically made with a coconut milk or tamarind-based broth, rice or egg noodles,and a variety of proteins and herbs.

Satay

Grilled skewered meat served with a delicious peanut sauce, commonly enjoyed as a snack or meal.

Roti Canai

Roti canai is a flaky, Indian-influenced flatbread from Southeast Asia, typically served with curry or dhal and enjoyed as a breakfast or snack.

Char Kuey Teow

Char kuey teow is a Malaysian and Singaporean stir,fried flat rice noodle dish made with prawns, cockles, bean sprouts, eggs, Chinese chives, and soy sauce, cooked over very high heat to create a smoky aroma.

Cendol

Cendol is a Southeast Asian iced dessert made of green pandan,flavoured rice,flour jelly, coconut milk, shaved ice, and palm sugar syrup.
